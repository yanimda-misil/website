A bedtime friend, ready for the night no one plans for.

Mışıl is a sleep and play companion for children aged 2 to 8. In a disaster, it helps meet a child’s basic needs.

Mışıl comes from “mışıl mışıl”, the Turkish way of saying a child is sleeping soundly. Yanımda means “by my side”.

Why a plush?

On 6 February 2023, the first of the earthquakes in Türkiye struck at 4:17 in the morning, while most children were asleep.

Emergency kits are usually made for adults. Mışıl starts from the friend a child already plays and sleeps with every day, and turns it into a pedagogical safety product. The aim is to prepare children for disasters without frightening them.

  • Nutrition

    Supports a child’s basic need for food in the first hours after a disaster.

  • Calling for help

    Gives a young child a simple way to call for help.

  • Emotional calming

    A familiar friend to hold helps a frightened child calm down.

Mışıl is at the concept stage. We are building a team across product design, engineering and intellectual property law to develop it.
